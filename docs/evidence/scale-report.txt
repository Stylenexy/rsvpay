What this means. Every unreplied gift makes the view read up to 100 of its giver's tokens. One gift from
a giver who holds one envelope costs about 0.4 TGas; each extra envelope that giver holds adds about 0.24 TGas.
The public RPC refuses a view somewhere between 750 and 1,200 TGas. With 100 rows per call, as the web
client asked until 9 November, a book stops ringing once a giver in it holds about 40 envelopes.
Nobody hosts 40 celebrations, but a stranger could make the envelopes on purpose (threat T21).
